I built Snare for @zama Developer Program Season 5.

Booby-trap a vault and log off. Strangers raid it while you're gone, and the chain checks every
route against traps nobody can see.

- traps stay encrypted forever unless they fire
- house vaults laid out by onchain encrypted randomness
- when a vault retires, the contract replays every raid to prove it was fair
